Project Proposal 

Relevance & Target Users 
GoBook provides a lightweight, high-concurrency booking solution for small to medium businesses. It is designed for managing time-sensitive resources such as co-working desks, medical appointments, and equipment rentals.

Planned Features 
User Management: JWT-based authentication and role-based access.
Resource Catalog: CRUD operations for bookable items with custom pricing.
Conflict-Free Booking: Logic to prevent overlapping schedules (double-booking).
Status Workflow: Tracking bookings through Pending, Confirmed, and Completed states.
